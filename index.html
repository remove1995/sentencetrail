How to play

  1. Roll the dice. Your token moves along the arrows.
  2. Your destination square reveals its category. Click that square or Reveal card to get a random verb. Squares you pass stay secret.
  3. Say a sentence using the category on the square: Present Simple, State verbs or Present Continuous.
  4. Your teacher clicks End turn. The next player takes over.
  5. There are 12 rounds. In each round, every player takes one turn. The furthest player wins. Reach FINISH first to win early. Equal positions mean a shared win.

State verbs: use a state meaning. For example, think = have an opinion. With have in the action categories, use an activity such as have lunch.
